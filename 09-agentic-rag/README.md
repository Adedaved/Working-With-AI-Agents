# 09. Agentic RAG

[Course home](../README.md)

## What you will learn

Retrieve the right evidence, assess its quality, and answer within what it supports.

## Start here

RAG supplies evidence from external documents instead of relying only on a model's trained knowledge. Agentic RAG decides how to retrieve, whether the evidence is sufficient and whether another step is justified. Chunking affects what can be retrieved; corrective retrieval handles weak results. Citation quality, source applicability and honest limits matter more than the number of retrieved passages.
