# AI agent ethics

[Chapter overview](README.md) | [Course home](../README.md)

## In plain English

Ethics asks whether the system's objectives and actions are appropriate for affected people. Agents can amplify a poor objective by acting on it repeatedly. Consider who benefits, who may be disadvantaged, what the system optimizes and which decisions require human judgment. Make these considerations concrete in the workflow rather than adding a generic statement at the end.

## Try it: original exercise

1. List the people affected by a proposed agent task.
2. Identify a metric that could reward undesirable behavior if used alone.
3. Define limits and a review point for consequential decisions.
4. Test cases where speed or cost conflicts with fairness or user interests.

## Check your understanding

Why can optimizing only ticket closure count be harmful?

**Answer checkpoint:** The agent might close tickets without actually resolving the user's problem.

## Common mistake

A high metric score is not a substitute for checking what behavior produced it.
