# AI agent sprawl

[Chapter overview](README.md) | [Course home](../README.md)

## In plain English

Sprawl is the uncontrolled growth of agents across teams without shared visibility or ownership. It can create duplicate workflows, inconsistent permissions, unnecessary cost and unclear accountability. Discover what exists before creating another agent. A register, approved templates and retirement process help maintain a manageable system. The objective is useful capability with visible control, rather than maximizing the number of agents.

## Try it: original exercise

1. Inventory existing agents and the business outcomes they support.
2. Identify duplicate purposes and abandoned integrations.
3. Assign owners and standardize access and release practices.
4. Retire obsolete agents and revoke unused permissions.

## Check your understanding

What is a practical first step when nobody knows how many agents are active?

**Answer checkpoint:** Build an inventory with owners, purposes, environments and access.

## Common mistake

An agent with no active user can still consume resources or retain credentials.
