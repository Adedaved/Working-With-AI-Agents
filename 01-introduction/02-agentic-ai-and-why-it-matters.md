# Agentic AI and why it matters

[Chapter overview](README.md) | [Course home](../README.md)

## In plain English

Agentic AI pursues objectives by choosing actions and adapting as results arrive. It can combine language-model flexibility with deterministic software tools. This is useful when a task requires interpretation and several connected steps. Its value comes from completed, correct work, not from autonomy alone. A useful evaluation compares time saved against review effort, errors, operating cost and impact. Some workflows need a fixed script rather than an LLM: select the simplest system that meets the requirements.

## Try it: original exercise

1. Choose a recurring task with a measurable output, such as a weekly ticket report.
2. Record how long the task and quality review currently take.
3. Give the agent a bounded version with a clear completion condition.
4. Compare correctness, review time and tool cost with the baseline.

## Check your understanding

An agent saves twenty minutes but needs thirty minutes of correction. Has it improved this workflow?

**Answer checkpoint:** Not on time alone. Improve the setup or choose a narrower task before claiming a productivity gain.

## Common mistake

Do not treat impressive demonstrations as evidence of reliable performance on your own data.
