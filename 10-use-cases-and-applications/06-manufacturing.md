# Manufacturing

[Chapter overview](README.md) | [Course home](../README.md)

## In plain English

Manufacturing agents can combine operational observations, work instructions and planning data to support quality or maintenance workflows. Their actions must respect safety and engineering limits. Begin with recommendations and evidence rather than direct machine changes. A useful maintenance brief identifies the observation, relevant procedure, uncertainty and the responsible reviewer.

## Try it: original exercise

1. Create synthetic equipment readings and an approved maintenance instruction.
2. Ask for anomalies and the procedure applicable to each observation.
3. Prepare a draft maintenance recommendation without changing equipment settings.
4. Have the responsible engineer validate the evidence and proposed action.

## Check your understanding

Can a model-generated setting be applied because it sounds technically plausible?

**Answer checkpoint:** No. Engineering limits, validated procedures and proper authorization govern machine changes.

## Common mistake

Operational optimization must not discard safety or quality constraints to improve a single metric.
