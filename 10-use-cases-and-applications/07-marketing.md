# Marketing

[Chapter overview](README.md) | [Course home](../README.md)

## In plain English

Marketing workflows combine research, analysis, content generation and coordination. Agents can prepare campaign briefs grounded in approved audience data and brand guidance. A useful output distinguishes observed facts from hypotheses and proposed creative choices. Publishing content or spending a campaign budget is a separate action from drafting. Measure effectiveness with appropriate evidence instead of treating generated enthusiasm as business performance.

## Try it: original exercise

1. Provide a synthetic campaign brief, audience description and brand guidance.
2. Ask for a draft message and an evidence-linked rationale.
3. Flag unverified product claims and proposed changes to the brief.
4. Review the draft and evaluation plan before publication or budget changes.

## Check your understanding

What should happen to an unsupported claim such as guaranteed savings?

**Answer checkpoint:** Remove it or seek approved evidence; do not publish it as fact.

## Common mistake

A persuasive draft is not proof of campaign performance.
