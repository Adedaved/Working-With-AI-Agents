# Human resources

[Chapter overview](README.md) | [Course home](../README.md)

## In plain English

HR agents can support policy lookup, onboarding materials, document preparation and workflow coordination. Use approved policy sources and explicit role requirements. An administrative assistant can assemble an onboarding checklist without becoming the decision-maker for employment outcomes. Keep personal information limited to what the task requires and review outputs for unsupported assumptions or inconsistent treatment.

## Try it: original exercise

1. Supply a synthetic new-hire role and an approved onboarding policy.
2. Ask for a role-specific checklist with policy references.
3. Flag prerequisites or decisions that require an HR owner.
4. Check the checklist against the policy and the supplied role details.

## Check your understanding

May the agent invent a qualification requirement absent from the role specification?

**Answer checkpoint:** No. Label it as a question or proposed change rather than an established requirement.

## Common mistake

Efficient document processing does not establish fairness or suitability for hiring decisions.
