# Sales

[Chapter overview](README.md) | [Course home](../README.md)

## In plain English

Sales agents can assemble account context, prepare meeting briefs and draft follow-up work. Ground each brief in permitted customer records and current source material. Distinguish recorded facts from assumptions about intent or likelihood to buy. Keep commitments and outreach reviewable. Success is a relevant, accurate deliverable that helps the salesperson act, rather than a longer description of the account.

## Try it: original exercise

1. Provide synthetic call notes, an opportunity record and an approved product sheet.
2. Ask for a one-page brief with facts, open questions and proposed next steps.
3. Check that no unsupported customer commitment or product claim was added.
4. Approve the follow-up draft before sending it or changing the opportunity.

## Check your understanding

May an agent report the customer agreed to buy when notes say only interested?

**Answer checkpoint:** No. Preserve the difference between interest and a commitment.

## Common mistake

Do not turn uncertain inferences into definitive CRM facts.
