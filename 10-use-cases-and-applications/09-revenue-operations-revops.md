# Revenue operations: RevOps

[Chapter overview](README.md) | [Course home](../README.md)

## In plain English

RevOps aligns sales, marketing and customer-success processes around shared revenue information. Agents can inspect records, detect inconsistencies and prepare follow-up tasks. Agree on definitions such as deal stage and stale record before automating decisions. Keep cross-system identifiers so the same customer or opportunity is not counted twice. A useful report shows the discrepancy and the evidence needed to resolve it.

## Try it: original exercise

1. Create synthetic opportunity and customer-success records with shared account IDs.
2. Define a stale opportunity rule and required stage fields.
3. Ask for a discrepancy report and proposed owner-specific follow-ups.
4. Review the findings before updating CRM stages or sending notifications.

## Check your understanding

What prevents the same customer being counted twice across systems?

**Answer checkpoint:** Stable identifiers, explicit matching rules and a check for duplicate records.

## Common mistake

A shared revenue goal does not mean all teams use identical field definitions.
