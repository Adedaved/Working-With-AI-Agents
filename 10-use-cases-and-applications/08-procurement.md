## In plain English

Procurement agents can help gather supplier information, compare offers and prepare review materials. State comparison criteria explicitly, including price basis, delivery terms and missing information. Separate a supplier shortlist from approval of a purchase. Use evidence-linked tables and explain the trade-offs rather than selecting whichever offer has the lowest visible number.

## Try it: original exercise

1. Create three synthetic offers with different prices, delivery times and conditions.
2. Specify comparison fields and any mandatory requirements.
3. Ask for a shortlist with evidence, missing terms and trade-offs.
4. Review the recommendation before creating a commitment or purchase order.

## Check your understanding

Why might the lowest quoted price fail the procurement criteria?

**Answer checkpoint:** It may exclude required services, miss delivery constraints or leave key terms unspecified.

## Common mistake

Do not compare totals built from different quantities, currencies or tax assumptions without normalization.
