## In plain English

Finance workflows can involve data gathering, reconciliations, discrepancy detection and evidence-linked reporting. Accuracy and traceability matter because a plausible summary can hide a wrong calculation or missing record. Use deterministic arithmetic for amounts and explicit rules for matching. Keep the agent's initial role in analysis and draft preparation unless a separate action has been authorized and controlled.

## Try it: original exercise

1. Create synthetic ledger and invoice records with known differences.
2. Ask the agent to match records and flag unmatched or inconsistent amounts.
3. Recompute totals using a spreadsheet formula or exact calculation tool.
4. Review the exception report before changing accounting records or initiating transactions.

## Check your understanding

Does a neatly formatted reconciliation prove every record was checked?

**Answer checkpoint:** No. Verify record coverage, matching rules and independently calculated totals.

## Common mistake

Financial analysis and execution are different tasks with different approval requirements.
