# Customer service

[Chapter overview](README.md) | [Course home](../README.md)

## In plain English

Customer-service agents can retrieve relevant policies, triage requests and prepare responses or approved actions. Give them current policy sources and a clear escalation rule. Separate explaining a refund policy from issuing a refund: those require different authority. Evaluate whether the proposed answer solves the actual customer problem, stays within policy and preserves the commitments already made.

## Try it: original exercise

1. Provide a synthetic customer request and a current service policy.
2. Ask for issue classification, relevant policy evidence and a draft response.
3. Escalate ambiguous eligibility or exceptions to a human reviewer.
4. Check the draft before sending and confirm any authorized action separately.

## Check your understanding

Why distinguish a refund draft from a refund transaction?

**Answer checkpoint:** One is proposed content; the other changes a real record or transfers value.

## Common mistake

An agent should not promise an action its tools or permissions cannot carry out.
