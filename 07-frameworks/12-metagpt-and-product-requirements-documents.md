# MetaGPT and product requirements documents

[Chapter overview](README.md) | [Course home](../README.md)

## In plain English

MetaGPT organizes specialized agents around structured work procedures. IBM's PRD automation guide applies this idea to producing a product requirements document using MetaGPT, Ollama and DeepSeek. A useful PRD distinguishes supplied requirements, assumptions and unresolved decisions. The agent team can help develop the document, but stakeholders must approve scope and priorities. Procedural structure reduces ambiguity only if the source requirements are correct.

## Try it: original exercise

1. Write a synthetic product brief with three requirements and two constraints.
2. Assign requirement extraction, drafting and review responsibilities.
3. Require acceptance criteria that map back to the original brief.
4. Reject invented features or mark them as proposals awaiting a stakeholder decision.

## Check your understanding

What should the PRD say about a feature no stakeholder requested?

**Answer checkpoint:** Mark it as a proposal or exclude it; do not present it as an approved requirement.

## Common mistake

A polished PRD can hide unresolved product decisions if assumptions are not labelled.
