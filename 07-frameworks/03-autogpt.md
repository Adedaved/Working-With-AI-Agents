## In plain English

AutoGPT illustrates high-level objectives broken into smaller steps and executed through available capabilities. The important learning point is control of a longer-running workflow: constrain tools, progress tracking and stopping conditions. A broad instruction such as grow my business hides too many decisions. Begin with a narrow task whose evidence and output you can inspect.

## Try it: original exercise

1. Replace a broad goal with a bounded task, such as compare three public documentation pages.
2. Specify the allowed sources and expected comparison fields.
3. Set a maximum run length and require a report of blocked steps.
4. Review each claim and recorded action before expanding the task.

## Check your understanding

Why is a broad goal difficult to evaluate?

**Answer checkpoint:** It leaves success criteria, scope and consequential trade-offs unspecified.

## Common mistake

Autonomous task creation can expand scope unless the task boundary is enforced.
