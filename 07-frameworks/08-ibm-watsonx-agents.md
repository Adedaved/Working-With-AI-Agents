# IBM watsonx agents

[Chapter overview](README.md) | [Course home](../README.md)

## In plain English

IBM's watsonx agent offering includes ways to configure business-oriented agents and integrate them with workflows. watsonx Orchestrate is associated with building and coordinating such agents; watsonx.ai supplies model and AI application capabilities used in many IBM labs. Know which service the lab actually requires. Prebuilt agents can reduce setup, while custom tools and evaluation still determine whether they suit your task.

## Try it: original exercise

1. Choose a narrow business workflow and identify the IBM service used in the relevant guide.
2. Inspect the agent's knowledge, tools and available integrations.
3. Configure a draft-only task and provide a representative example.
4. Evaluate real outputs before granting additional actions.

## Check your understanding

**Answer checkpoint:** No. Validate them against your sources, permissions and quality requirements.
